# V10: "Studio" layout with colour themes

All your content, progress and features (including CISA) are unchanged. The login page is untouched.

## Layout
- **Sidebar.** The navigation is a vertical sidebar on the left again. It is grouped into Learn, Track and Tools, and the active page is highlighted.
- **Sidebar footer.** The bottom of the sidebar holds your account, sync status, the colour swatches, Light/Dark and Sign out.
- **Phones.** The sidebar becomes a top bar with a ☰ menu.
- **Page titles.** Each page title sits on a band of the theme colour to the right of the sidebar, and the first cards overlap the band.
- **More space.** There is more room around everything: wider margins, larger card padding, and more space between rows and between menu items. Cards have finer borders and softer shadows.
- **Type.** Inter for headings and text, at lighter weights.

## Dashboard banner
- **Left:** your next module with a progress bar, and "Start/Continue module" and "All modules" buttons.
- **Right:** your Technical Academy progress ring, and a CISA card with days to the exam and topics covered.
- **Bottom:** the five levels, shown as thin progress lines.
- **Row below:** lessons learned, quiz accuracy, day streak and hours studied this week.

## Colour themes
Pick a colour with the swatches at the bottom of the sidebar. Your choice is remembered on this device and works in light and dark mode.
- **Navy & Gold (default):** navy sidebar, blue band, gold highlights.
- **Forest & Mint:** deep green with mint highlights.
- **Royal Violet:** deep violet with lavender highlights.
- **Sunset:** the earlier orange-to-pink look.
